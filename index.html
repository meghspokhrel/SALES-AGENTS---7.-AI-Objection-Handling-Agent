Two deployment modes

AI can speak for the process — or work beside your salesperson.

Different businesses need different levels of automation.

Autonomous mode

AI handles the conversation.

Useful for first-stage sales conversations where approved messaging and business rules are clear.

  • Detect common objections
  • Ask clarifying questions
  • Provide approved information
  • Monitor buying intent
  • Escalate when human judgment is needed
Sales copilot mode

AI coaches your salesperson.

Recommended for higher-value or complex sales where the human remains in control of the conversation.

  • Detect objection in real time
  • Explain what may be behind it
  • Recommend response strategy
  • Generate suggested replies
  • Help the salesperson avoid premature discounting
